Four Outfits is Plenty

Asia is the perfect place to go super UL. When you are hiking in the wilderness most of your backpack is your sleeping bag, shelter, and food. But when you're urban traveling all you really need is extra clothes. That's it. And so that's all I really took. This is my packing list for 6 countries over the course of two months in SE Asia and India.

Patagonia Black Hole 25L. I really love the black hole line. They are extra durable and very simple. One big compartment and I have a nice blue one. On my first trip to India I used a Fjallraven pack but it had problems: it was a bit too small, had a wimpy laptop sleeve, and was separated into two main pockets.

Himalayas Make Me Barf

I did not know India stretched north enough to reach the Himalayas. But it does. Me and my friends took a looong bus ride from the valley up 6,000 feet to a city just below the famous towering mountains. The HIMALAYAS!

The streets are narrow. They were built four hundred years ago, and because of that only designed with people and yaks in mind. But four centuries later and the streets are infested with cars. They zoom up and down the winding mountain roads leaving no room at all for people. It's annoying constantly being honked at. If you're a person get used to walking in the ditch. Or being hit by a vehicle.
